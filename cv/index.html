Work

  •
    Graduate Researcher, AI for Biology and Protein Design
    Diwakar Shukla Group, University of Illinois Urbana-Champaign
    Generative AI systems that propose scientific hypotheses and designs.
    • Learned ranker for protein-ligand pose prediction that reaches 86.0% top-1 accuracy on PoseBusters v2.
    • Protein language models (ESM2) adapted with LoRA fine-tuning.
    • Tests of whether LLM representations and outputs can be trusted, using acceptance guards and null controls (NeurIPS 2026 XAI4Science workshop).
    • Methods for automatic scientific discovery: deciding which measurement separates competing mechanistic explanations of the same data.
  • Graduate Researcher, Whole-Cell Modeling
    Luthey-Schulten Group, University of Illinois Urbana-Champaign
    Spatially resolved whole-cell simulation of baker's yeast (Saccharomyces cerevisiae).
    • Built the first 4D hybrid RDME-ODE simulations of the yeast galactose switch on the GPU-based Lattice Microbes platform, with cell geometry taken from cryo-electron tomograms.
    • Benchmarked the multi-GPU solver under different spatial decompositions.
    • Platform talk and session co-chair, Systems Biophysics, Biophysical Society Annual Meeting 2026.
