Projects

  • 2026 – Present
    Computational Protein Engineering: Form II Rubisco (Rhodospirillum rubrum)
    An end-to-end computational pipeline for engineering a carbon-fixing enzyme, from predictive models to designs a collaborating wet lab can test.
    • Trained and benchmarked sequence- and structure-based models on deep mutational scanning data; established calibration and error floors before using predictions to rank candidates.
    • Ran molecular dynamics, free-energy, and quantum-chemical calculations on the catalytic site; built decoy and radial controls to test whether a proposed structural descriptor is actually site-local.
    • Designed de novo binders and redesigned active sites with generative backbone models; converted the surviving designs into ordered constructs, matched controls, and assay protocols for experimental validation.
  • Predicting Protein Aggregation Propensity with Protein Language Models
    A sequence-based machine-learning framework for aggregation propensity, from whole-protein prediction to residue-level aggregation-prone regions. First-author manuscript in preparation.
    • Built a protein language model-based predictor of aggregation propensity, trained on a published high-throughput stability library and extended to residue-level aggregation-prone region detection.
    • Designed nested and external validation protocols; benchmarked against public curated aggregation databases and training-free biophysical baselines.
    • Ran in-silico deep mutational scanning on an antibody Fab; coordinated experimental validation (DLS, BLI) with collaborators.
