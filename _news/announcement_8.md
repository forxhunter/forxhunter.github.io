Released [MetaCarto 2]({% link _projects/3_project.md %}): every one of the 108 BiGG models drawn as curator-style Escher maps, plus each whole model on a single canvas, with no text overlapping anything. [Browse the maps in the viewer](https://forxhunter.github.io/escher/), [run it on your own model](https://github.com/forxhunter/MetaCarto), or read the [preprint on bioRxiv](https://doi.org/10.64898/2026.09.19.752882). :world_map:
